Version: 3.2.0
Date: 30-09-26
  Major Features:
    - Alien tech tree rework. Every technology of the tree is now script-only (no lab research),
      visible in the technology screen and depends on the lab technology "Resonance synthesizer"
      (the bio chamber keeps its own prerequisites: the alien science pack needs it). A node can
      only be bought when the prerequisites of its technology are researched.
    - Tree layout: tier 1 gets the conduit instead of the resonance synthesizer (which stays a lab
      technology), tier 4 adds the data center and the crystal radio station, tier 5 the void engine
      (same costs as the other nodes of their tier).
    - Alien knowledge payment: missing knowledge is paid from the inventory in the priority
      knowledge -> alien resonance packs (10 knowledge each, surplus is refunded) -> resonance data
      (10 per knowledge). The confirm dialog shows exactly what is consumed.
    - Salvaging broken ruins: mining, deconstructing or destroying an unrepaired alien artifact on
      Gaia grants 10 % of the repair reward. Salvaged ruins no longer return themselves but a small
      random amount of high tier resources (high energy crystals, magnets, alien resin, ...).
      No more knowledge is granted once the whole tree is unlocked.
    - Unrepaired ruins are immune to lightning.
    - Ancient conduit (ei-conduit-gaia): a non-craftable, indestructible lightning rod that replaces
      the fulgoran ruin attractors on Gaia (new chunks, existing Gaia surfaces are migrated).
      Until the conduit is unlocked players have to build next to them.
    - Radio station (electricity age, 100 kW): wireless circuit network channels on one surface.
      Any signal is a channel, one transmitter and any number of receivers per channel.
    - Crystal radio station (alien tier 4, 10 MW): the same for every surface of the game.
  Changes:
    - Resonance synthesizer recipe multiplies data: 1 resonance data + 100 morphium + 50 computing
      power -> 2 resonance data (was 3 from nothing).
    - Data center recipes always produce 1 pack and cost 1 computing power per unique component of
      the pack's recursive production chain (loop-safe, 100 as fallback for too large chains).
    - The "Vent dirty water" recipe is unlocked by the purifier technology.
    - The gate technology description no longer promises travel (the gate only moves items).
    - Unified mod prefix: every "ei_" name is now "ei-" (entities, items, recipes, technologies,
      settings, sprites, styles, GUI elements). Saves are migrated (migrations/3.2.0.json); the
      renamed settings "ei-ticks_per_full_update", "ei-trains_max_updates_per_tick",
      "ei-fueler_max_updates_per_tick" and "ei-fueler_range" return to their defaults once.
  Removed:
    - Age progression gate ("research N % of the previous age"): hidden dummy technologies, the
      "counts for age progression" overlay and the InformaTron "age progress" remnants. Ages stay as
      technology metadata.
    - Drone port / drone remnants and the unused gate.teleport_player.
    - Space destinations remnants (InformaTron page, styles, black hole data item, rocket silo GUI
      texts, satellites and planet rocks locale).
    - Orphaned locale entries of prototypes that no longer exist.
  Bugfixes:
    - InformaTron "cranes and belts" page referenced the non-existing item ei-big-inserter.
    - Metalworks level overlays and a few item descriptions referenced wrong names.
  Locale:
    - Russian: "Морфий" -> "Морфиум", "Дальняя станция" -> "Дальстанция".
    - Short flavor descriptions for every entity, item, fluid, recipe, technology, tile and the
      planet that had none (English and Russian).

Version: 3.1.0
Date: 29-09-26
  Major Features:
    - Alien tech tree reactivated (InformaTron page "Alien tech tree", 5 tiers). Repairing an alien
      artifact grants "alien knowledge" points (100 per repair) that unlock technologies instead of
      researching them in a lab. Tiers 1-3 restore original (known as EI knowledge system) nodes, tier 4 "Resonant
      computation" and tier 5 "Threshold engineering" are new and also consume resonance data /
      alien resonance packs from the inventory. Technologies researched in a lab count as unlocked.
    - Resonance data: every repaired artifact spills 2-4 pieces. The new resonance synthesizer
      (alien tier 1 or regular research) synthesizes it from morphium and computing power.
    - Alien resonance pack: second alien science tier, crafted in the resonance synthesizer and only
      on Gaia (recipe surface conditions).
    - Data center: crafts any science pack of the big lab (vanilla, Space Age, other mods and the
      alien packs) from computing power only, 10x slower than regular production. Recipes are
      generated automatically in data-final-fixes. Graphics: Hurricane046 cybernetics facility.
    - Conduit: lightning attractor that turns 80 % of every captured strike into electricity
      (40 MJ buffer, 5 MW output). Unlocked with Gaia. Graphics: Hurricane046 conduit.
    - Void rift generator (alien tier 5): carves a 5x5 out-of-map rift south of itself for void
      engines and restores the original terrain and resources when removed.
    - Gaia storm EMP: lightning strikes on Gaia that miss every lightning attractor disable machines,
      inserters and combinators around the impact for 5 seconds (map setting "Gaia storm EMP").
    - Gate hub rule: every gate jump goes through Gaia. A gate outside of Gaia can only lead to Gaia,
      a gate on Gaia to every other surface. Invalid exits of old saves are reset.
    - Platform requests scanner (orbital combinator) consumes 1 computing power per minute through
      a hidden data port while an orbiting platform has requests; without it the signals freeze
      and a warning icon is shown.
    - Underground data cable (alien tier 4).
    - Five new hand-made Gaia ruin presets (shrine, grove, outpost, sanctuary, monument).
  Bugfixes:
    - Restored Alien (knowledge system) tech tree: the tree was empty, the InformaTron page "alien" did not exist, the currency
      was never granted, tier unlocking was hardcoded to 3 tiers and never called, button sprites
      pointed to non-existing sprites and the node state always used the "player" force.
    - Artifact spawner: 5 presets could never spawn (rarity "temp" / "very-rare" typos, duplicate
      table keys "accu-site_5" and "beacon-site_small-2" overwrote each other).
    - InformaTron: gate and repair pages were unreachable (dependency on the never-set
      storage.ei.knowledge), empty "surface patches" headings removed, the "tech" section and the
      space destinations page restored (text updated for Space Age), "Informatron wiki updated"
      messages were never sent and used a non-existing sprite.
    - Alien confirm dialog used missing locale keys (renamed knowledge-confirm-* keys).
    - Missing locale: technology names of modules 4-6, solar panels 2/3 and crystal accumulator,
      gate remote item name, black hole start button caption.
    - Void engine and burner quarry used the Factorio 1.x "module_specification" field that 2.0
      ignores (void engine had no module slots).
  Changes:
    - "No triggers" setting no longer converts script-only (alien tree) technologies into lab
      technologies.
    - Beacon overload and storm EMP never re-enable a machine the other system still disables.
  Locale:
    - New complete Russian translation (locale/ru), English texts for all new content.

Version: 3.0.0
Date: 25-09-26
  Major Features:
    - Multiplayer desync fixes. All runtime state now lives in the synchronised storage table:
      the entity update scheduler used a plain Lua variable (not saved) to pick the updater of the
      current tick, so a client that joined a game ran different updates than the server.
      Verified with Factorio heavy mode (original: mismatch every 10 ticks, now: none).
    - Round-robin updaters (beacons, pipes, neutron collectors, matter stabilizers, orbital
      combinators, fuelers, gates, EM trains/chargers) no longer call next() with removed keys,
      which crashed or desynced freshly loaded clients ("invalid key to 'next'").
  Bugfixes:
    - Black hole generator: the GUI stopped updating after save/load and the startup state was lost
      as soon as the mass reached zero. The "startup" state is now stored explicitly, the generator
      is updated every tick (stage 1 takes the documented 60 seconds, extractors deliver up to
      100 GW each) and the GUI refreshes reliably. Existing generators are migrated.
    - Black hole generator: mining a generator that still contained items crashed the game.
    - Black hole generator: opening an unregistered generator crashed the GUI.
    - Dedicated servers crashed when no player had joined yet (EM train buffs read game.players[1]).
    - Typing "/gaia" in chat crashed the game for everyone. It is now an admin-only teleport.
    - Opening the induction matrix console could crash the game on the periodic GUI update.
    - Induction matrix: several crashes when cores/tiles were removed or a matrix had no stats yet.
    - Neutron collectors next to fusion reactors always had 0 % efficiency (wrong recipe name
      format when reading the reactor fuel mode).
    - EM chargers: researched efficiency levels made the energy consumption negative (chargers
      produced energy). Efficiency now follows the research levels 10 % .. 90 %.
    - EM chargers: partial charges drained the charger completely, the range toggle button of the
      EM train window did nothing, charger/train queues collected duplicates after every update.
    - EM train and charger glow settings could not be disabled (wrong setting names).
    - Gate: remote exit selection is now per player (two players could overwrite each other,
      using a leftover remote crashed the game), a player leaving during the selection gets his
      character back on rejoin, items waiting in a removed gate are dropped instead of deleted,
      messages go to the gate owner's force instead of spamming everyone.
    - Fueler: settings are stored per entity (unit number) instead of by position, quality and
      spoilage of fuel/ammo are preserved, turrets are always resupplied, removed fuelers no longer
      break the update queue. The Warp Drive Machine surface-change workaround was replaced by
      proper handling of cloned entities.
    - Matter stabilizer range/connection previews are drawn per player (selecting a stabilizer
      cleared the previews of all other players).
    - Beacon overload: vanilla beacons and newly built machines are now checked as well, a beacon
      that is being removed is not counted anymore, and only machines disabled by the overload are
      re-enabled.
    - Crystal accumulator: the Gaia variant (double output) is used on Gaia again (surface name
      mismatch), and it is swapped back / mined as the regular accumulator outside of Gaia.
    - Artifact spawner: stale queue entries on deleted surfaces crashed the game, the recording
      tools used the removed game.write_file API.
    - Chat messages with emoji were garbled by the colour gradient (UTF-8 aware now).
    - Several helper functions (recipe_add, recipe_remove, remove_prerequisite, tech ingredient
      de-duplication) skipped entries or never matched the Factorio 2.0 ingredient format.
    - Level overlays for vanilla machine icons are shown (they silently failed for icons without
      an explicit icon_size).
  Changes:
    - Data cables use their own pipe connection category: they only connect to data cables and to
      the data connections of computers, never to regular pipes. Computing power connections of the
      computer core, small simulator and quantum computer can not be connected to regular pipes, so
      regular pipes can no longer carry computing power. Regular pipes that still contain computing
      power explode (this check did not run at all without copper beacons before).
    - Batteries from scrap recycling stay at the vanilla 4 % (scrap results are also filtered for
      items removed by other mods).
    - Removed the duplicate steam crusher recipe for crushed iron ("ei-crushed-iron-plate"), machines
      using it are migrated to "ei-crushed-iron".
    - Krastorio 2 Spaced Out: K2 sand is merged into EI sand, every crusher (steam, EI, K2) can use
      both crushing recipe categories. Leftover K2 sand can be converted by hand.
    - Krastorio 2 Spaced Out: "Imersium processing" no longer requires the imersite tech pack it
      unlocks. A general guard removes science packs from technologies that unlock them.
    - Gaia map generation is built from a fresh copy of the vanilla Fulgora settings and enforced in
      data-final-fixes, so mods changing Fulgora or injecting tiles into every planet can no longer
      turn Gaia into an endless grass field.
    - "/esi-gaia-reborn" is admin only, runs as a state machine (surface deletion is deferred by
      the engine) and gives up after 5 failed regenerations instead of looping forever.
    - Remelting loops (plate -> molten metal -> plate, also via mechanical parts) never receive
      productivity, including productivity research, so foundry/module productivity can no longer
      create infinite copper, iron, steel, gold or lead.
    - Removed the rocket silo "cheat mode" hack: opening a rocket silo gave the player cheat mode
      (free instant crafting) and removed cheat mode from admins that had it enabled.
    - Loaders only snap to belts/containers when built by hand; loaders from blueprints keep their
      blueprint orientation.
    - Debloat setting is applied once more at the very end (age packs assigned late were missed).
    - Removed the unused "esi-gaia-reborn" custom input and empty script files.
  Scripting:
    - Control stage reorganised: one scheduler (tick based), central GUI click dispatch (including
      all "open Informatron" buttons), shared helpers in scripts/control/util.lua, storage
      initialisation and migrations in one place, detailed documentation in every module.
    - Removed accidental global variables in control and data stage scripts.
